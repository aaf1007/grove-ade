## How the stage never sees the fix

- The stage reviews a fresh repo made with `git archive`: one commit for the branch's base on main, one for the branch just before the fix. It shares no objects with grove and has no remote.
- The harness refuses a stage repo that holds the fix commit, a remote, or a fixed file.
- Trees that hold the fix or its test, for the proof and the gates, live in their own folders and are deleted as soon as that step ends. The stage may not read the ones other cases are using, nor any other case's folder in the run.
- The stage session may only read and run `git diff`, `log`, `show` and `status`. Reads of the grove checkout and of Claude's session logs of grove are denied, and so are `gh` and MCP servers.
- After each stage the harness searches its whole transcript for the fix sha, the grove path, the answer key folders and other cases' folders. A hit marks the stage `leaked` and it does not count.
- Only the judge sees the fix and its test, in a separate session with no tools.
